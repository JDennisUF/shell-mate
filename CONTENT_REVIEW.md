# Checklist content

The catalog includes 89 tarstones, 53 beacons, 46 bosses, and 15 Rotten Autumn dungeons. Ghost Beacons and their dungeons are separate checks. Totals count checklist entries.

Tarstones include 75 original entries, two additional Fragile stones (Glimpse Stone and Egon's Stone), and 12 Rotten Autumn entries. Wellspring Stone and Forsaken Vow are represented without an acquisition route until one is documented. Alternate names such as Forsaken Vow Stone are searchable aliases rather than extra checklist entries.

Source links are available inside each route. Tarstone catalog order follows the item inventory, grouped by type.

## Updating content

Edit `data.js` to add entries or correct details reported by users. Never rename or reuse an existing `id`, so corrections preserve saved progress. Use `aliases` for alternate spellings and `nearestBeaconId` and `relatedIds` for links. Keep source links with the relevant entries and run the catalog checks after editing.
