# Checklist content

The catalog includes 89 tarstones, 53 beacons, 46 bosses, and 52 dungeons: 37 base-game dungeons and 15 Rotten Autumn dungeons. Ghost Beacons and their dungeons are separate checks. Totals count checklist entries.

The base-game dungeon routes follow the overworld dungeon list and include the tutorial’s Derelict Hideout. Each dungeon is a separate progress check; Beacon routes link back to their nearest Beacon.

Tarstones include 75 original entries, two additional Fragile stones (Glimpse Stone and Egon's Stone), and 12 Rotten Autumn entries. Wellspring Stone and Forsaken Vow are represented without an acquisition route until one is documented. Alternate names such as Forsaken Vow Stone are searchable aliases rather than extra checklist entries.

Source links are available inside each route. Tarstone catalog order follows the item inventory, grouped by type.

## Updating content

Edit `data.js` to add entries or correct details reported by users. Never rename or reuse an existing `id`, so corrections preserve saved progress. Use `aliases` for alternate spellings and `nearestBeaconId` and `relatedIds` for links. Keep source links with the relevant entries and run the catalog checks after editing.

Tarstone details now show each stone’s general effect above its Level 1–3 upgrade descriptions, with a direct Fextralife page link on each sourced entry. Fextralife lists Bloodoath Stone’s unupgraded base effect at Level 1 and its three upgrades at Levels 2–4; the app keeps its existing three upgrade descriptions and shows the base effect separately. Fextralife has no content on the Forsaken Vow page, so that description remains based on the existing catalog sources.
